Make the site feel more powerful, energetic, and positive while integrating the two uploaded RDX Forma posters as useful branded content rather than decorative images.

## Changes
- Add both uploaded posters through the site’s optimized media flow, preserving their original branding and readability.
- Rework the membership area around the official pricing poster:
  - Present the poster prominently alongside a clean, mobile-readable summary of the listed membership and personal-training prices.
  - Include the 5% GST note clearly.
  - Replace the current generic monthly plan pricing so visitors do not see conflicting prices.
- Add a dedicated “Train Strong. Train Respectfully.” section for the SOP poster:
  - Feature the original poster with a concise, positive summary of key operating hours, safety, hygiene, equipment, and conduct expectations.
  - Provide an image-view control so visitors can inspect the full poster without crowding the page.
- Strengthen the page’s energy with sharper red accents, bolder section headings, dynamic entry motion, and a branded “LIFT. MOVE. EVOLVE.” transition band.
- Keep the existing dark luxury style, slideshow, video, trainers, programs, and smart-pass experience intact.
- Ensure the new content is easy to read and tap on mobile, including the current narrow phone view.

## Technical details
